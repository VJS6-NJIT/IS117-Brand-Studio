**Research date:** November 9, 2026

## 1. Research Purpose

The purpose of this research is to investigate how established clothing brands present everyday T-shirts, communicate their value, and appeal to their customers.

The findings will inform the positioning, target audience, visual presentation, and website specifications for COMMON THREAD.

This research builds on the existing COMMON THREAD Brand Studio project. Previously approved brand decisions are retained as the starting point, but assumptions about customers and purchasing motivations will be tested rather than treated as established facts.

## 2. Existing Brand Foundation

The following decisions were established during the November 2 Brand Studio work:

## 3. Competitive Research

### Competitor 1: UNIQLO

**Source:** https://www.uniqlo.com/us/en/products/E465755-000/00

**Source type:** Official product page  
**Access date:** November 9, 2026

**Observations:**

- Identifies the fabric composition as 70% cotton and 30% polyester.
- Describes AIRism fabric with quick-drying functionality.
- Identifies the fit as relaxed, with a boxy silhouette.
- Provides size options from XXS through XXL.
- Explains that the shirt can be worn on its own or as a base layer.

**What this suggests:**

Customers benefit from understanding what makes a product useful. COMMON THREAD should communicate fabric composition, fit, care instructions, and practical benefits clearly.

**Potential opportunity:**

Present useful product information in a friendly, approachable way without overwhelming customers with technical language.

**Limitation:**

UNIQLO's use of fabric technology and functional benefits may be difficult for a smaller brand to compete with directly.

### Competitor 2: Everlane

**Source:** https://www.everlane.com/products/womens-organic-cotton-box-cut-pocket-tee-white

**Source type:** Official product page  
**Access date:** November 9, 2026

**Observations:**

- Identifies the material as 100% organic cotton.
- Describes the fit as relaxed, with a slightly cropped length.
- Provides a size guide with body and garment measurements.
- Includes product storytelling and customer reviews.
- Communicates product details in a conversational tone.

**What this suggests:**

Transparency and useful product descriptions can help customers make informed purchasing decisions.

**Potential opportunity:**

COMMON THREAD can combine clear material and sizing information with its existing friendly and inclusive brand voice.

**Limitation:**

Everlane's product presentation and material messaging create expectations that COMMON THREAD should not imitate without supporting evidence.

### Competitor 3: Cotton On

**Source:** https://cottonon.com/US/everyday-fit-t-shirt/3613863-01.html

**Source type:** Official product page  
**Access date:** November 9, 2026

**Observations:**

- Identifies the shirt as a men's Everyday Fit T-Shirt.
- Describes its crew neck, loose fit, everyday length, and relaxed short sleeves.
- Identifies the material as 100% cotton.
- Provides chest circumference and front-length measurements across sizes 2XS–2XL.

**What this suggests:**

Customers benefit from simple product descriptions, understandable sizing, and straightforward shopping experiences.

**Potential opportunity:**

COMMON THREAD can offer a similarly straightforward shopping experience while emphasizing its own visual identity and brand personality.

**Limitation:**

Promotional pricing is not necessarily an appropriate positioning strategy for COMMON THREAD.

### Homepage and Visual Presentation Research

**Research date:** November 9, 2026

The following observations are based on the official homepage content and navigation.

#### UNIQLO Homepage

**Source:** https://www.uniqlo.com/us/en/

- Organizes navigation around clothing categories, collections, and specialized fabric technologies.
- Uses the LifeWear concept to connect clothing with everyday activities.
- Features shopping categories, best sellers, seasonal collections, and inspiration content.
- Communicates simplicity and accessibility through its brand messaging.

**What this suggests for COMMON THREAD:**

Use straightforward navigation and organize the storefront around the customer's shopping needs. Everyday versatility should be communicated consistently throughout the homepage.

#### Everlane Homepage

**Source:** https://www.everlane.com/

- Combines shopping categories with editorial brand storytelling.
- Promotes responsible materials, product longevity, and craftsmanship.
- Provides dedicated sections explaining sustainability and factory practices.
- Connects product quality with the company's brand values.

**What this suggests for COMMON THREAD:**

A clear brand story can strengthen product presentation. COMMON THREAD should explain its identity and values without making unsupported claims about sustainability or manufacturing.

#### Cotton On Homepage

**Source:** https://cottonon.com/US/

- Organizes shopping around Women, Men, Kids, and product categories.
- Features prominent promotions and seasonal campaigns.
- Uses product imagery and category shortcuts to encourage shopping.
- Includes Cotton On Foundation messaging alongside commercial content.

**What this suggests for COMMON THREAD:**

Keep product discovery simple and accessible. Community messaging can contribute to brand identity, but the shopping experience should remain clear rather than becoming dominated by promotions.

**Visual research limitation:**

The homepage content and navigation were examined, but detailed visual judgments about photography composition, exact color usage, typography, and spacing still require direct screenshot inspection. These elements should not be treated as fully verified until manually reviewed.

**Important research limitation:** The three examples use different product lines and audience presentations. Their features should not be treated as a controlled comparison of equivalent products.

**Product comparison limitation:** These products are not identical comparison samples. The UNIQLO and Everlane examples are women's T-shirts, while the Cotton On example is a men's T-shirt. Their fit descriptions, sizing systems, and intended product presentations may differ. The comparison is therefore intended to identify useful approaches to product communication and shopping experience, not to establish which brand offers the best equivalent T-shirt.

Further visual inspection of the homepages, navigation, lifestyle photography, and product layouts is needed before finalizing the visual comparison.

## 5. Two Positioning Hypotheses

### Position A: Everyday Comfort and Versatility

**Positioning concept:** Clothing that fits naturally into everyday life.

- Primary benefit: Comfort and versatility.
- Customer priority: Practical everyday clothing.
- Brand character: Simple, reliable, approachable.
- Visual direction: Minimalist layouts, neutral colors, and clear product photography.

**Strength:** Communicates a practical reason to purchase.

**Risk:** Could make the brand appear generic if it lacks a recognizable personality.

### Position B: Personal Identity and Belonging

**Positioning concept:** Different lives. One common thread.

- Primary benefit: Connection and self-expression.
- Customer priority: Authenticity, individuality, and inclusion.
- Brand character: Welcoming, expressive, community-oriented.
- Visual direction: Inclusive lifestyle photography and relatable messaging.

**Strength:** Creates an emotional connection and a recognizable brand identity.

**Risk:** Emotional messaging alone may not explain the product's practical value.

### Preliminary Positioning Recommendation

Combine the strongest elements of both positions.

The primary commercial proposition should be everyday comfort and versatility, supported by belonging and personal expression as the emotional differentiator.

This approach preserves the existing COMMON THREAD identity while giving customers a practical reason to purchase.

This recommendation remains a hypothesis pending peer and customer feedback.

## 6. Two Persona Hypotheses

These personas are fictional research hypotheses, not actual customers or verified demographic findings.

### Persona A: The Everyday Essential Shopper

**Profile:** An adult who wants comfortable and dependable clothing for everyday activities.

- Priorities: Comfort, fit, versatility, and reasonable pricing.
- Frustrations: Unclear sizing, inconsistent quality, and complicated shopping.
- Purchasing motivation: Finding a reliable T-shirt that works with different outfits.
- Website needs: Clear sizing, fabric information, product photographs, and simple checkout.

**Assumption to test:** Practical product benefits matter more to this customer than brand identity.

### Persona B: The Individual Expression Shopper

**Profile:** An adult who values individuality, authenticity, and feeling connected to a brand.

- Priorities: Personal style, inclusivity, authenticity, and brand character.
- Frustrations: Generic branding, unrealistic advertising, and limited representation.
- Purchasing motivation: Supporting a brand whose personality and values feel relatable.
- Website needs: Lifestyle photography, brand storytelling, relatable messaging, and accessible products.

**Assumption to test:** Emotional connection influences this customer's purchasing decisions.

## 7. Primary Audience Recommendation

**Provisional target audience:**

Adults who value comfortable, versatile, approachable everyday clothing and want products that fit naturally into their personal style.

### Reasoning

1. This audience aligns with the practical product benefits observed across the three competitors.
2. It builds on COMMON THREAD's existing emphasis on comfort and everyday versatility.
3. It gives the brand a clear commercial proposition.
4. It allows inclusion and belonging to distinguish the brand emotionally.

The audience remains broad and requires further validation. We should investigate whether a more specific customer segment would produce a clearer positioning strategy.

## 8. Visual Style and Brand Character

COMMON THREAD will continue exploring its previously selected Modernist direction.

**Visual characteristics:**

- Warm neutral color palette.
- Clean typography and clear visual hierarchy.
- Generous spacing.
- Simple product presentation.
- Natural, inclusive lifestyle photography.
- Consistent and accessible navigation.

**Brand character:**

Friendly, inclusive, straightforward, approachable, and authentic.

**Design reasoning:**

The Modernist direction supports simplicity and clarity. Lifestyle photography and welcoming messaging provide personality and help prevent the minimalist presentation from feeling impersonal.

## 9. Honest Persuasion

COMMON THREAD will use Unity and Liking as its primary persuasion principles.

| Principle | Application |
|---|---|
| Unity | Communicate belonging without suggesting everyone must share one identity. |
| Liking | Use relatable photography and approachable language. |
| Transparency | Provide accurate fabric, sizing, and pricing information. |
| Credibility | Avoid unsupported quality, durability, or sustainability claims. |
| Customer confidence | Provide clear product details and return information. |

The website should help customers make informed decisions rather than pressure them into purchasing.
