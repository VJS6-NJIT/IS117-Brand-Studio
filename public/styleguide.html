00 / Brand direction

Room to be yourself.

Approved brand purpose, archetypes, personality, and design direction.

Brand and tagline

COMMON THREAD

A versatile essential. A place to belong.

Archetypes

Primary / Everyman: Everyone belongs.

Secondary / Creator: Express who you are.

Selected direction

Modernist

“It better communicates comfort, simplicity, and belonging.”

Mission

Create approachable everyday clothing that brings people together through comfort, versatility, and shared identity.

Vision

Build a welcoming lifestyle brand where people from different backgrounds can feel connected without sacrificing individuality.

The main SVG uses a sage thread accent and charcoal wordmark. The light and reversed monochrome SVGs preserve the same artwork and use approved colors for contrast. Shared assets: assets/wordmark.svg, assets/wordmark-monochrome.svg, and assets/wordmark-reversed.svg. The crossing thread represents connection and belonging; its two open ends represent individuality.

02 / Color

A grounded
palette.

Approved color names, values, and current storefront roles.

Warm Ivory

#EAE9E1

Storefront and guide page backgrounds.

Charcoal

#30332E

Primary text and filled actions.

Stone

#A5A18F

Rules, borders, and the product surface.

Sand

#B7A28B

Approved supporting palette tone; no storefront component currently uses it.

Sage

#66745E

Restrained thread-mark and hero-edge accent.

Charcoal supports legible contrast, while Warm Ivory, Stone, and Sand keep the palette grounded. Sage provides a calm accent. Check color contrast for the actual text, surfaces, focus indicators, and states.

Welcoming words.
No exaggeration.

Friendly, confident, conversational, inclusive, straightforward, and authentic.

Voice traits
