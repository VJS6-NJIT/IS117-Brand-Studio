    Direction choices

    • A deliberate two-column grid aligns image and information.
    • DM Sans carries the headline and controls; Lora stays an accent.
    • Fine rules and restrained Sage accents keep price and sizes easy to scan.

    Intended audience effect (hypothesis): The clear hierarchy and functional structure may feel approachable and dependable, making the offer easier to understand without overwhelming the customer.

    Risk: The restrained system could feel too neutral and understate the Creator archetype's individuality.
