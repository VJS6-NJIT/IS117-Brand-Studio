    DESIGN STUDY / POSTMODERNIST

    Reference notes

    Historical works are cited as references, not reproduced or presented as COMMON THREAD artwork.

    Concrete Stereo

    Ron Arad, 1983. V&A, object W.7-2011.

    The viewed stereo places separated components in rough concrete blocks. This preview borrows the material contrast and juxtaposition, not the object or its image.

    V&A, What is Postmodernism?

    Super lamp prototype

    Martine Bedin (for Memphis), 1981; made by Fausto Celati. V&A, object M.1-2011.

    The viewed lamp combines a wheeled blue body with repeated colored bulb sockets. This preview borrows playful repetition and asymmetry, not the lamp's shape.

    V&A, What is Postmodernism?

    Direction choices

    • An offset image frame and overlapping color planes make the composition asymmetrical.
    • DM Sans and Lora mix voices while the words remain identical to the Modernist preview.
    • Repeated geometry and Sage/Sand blocks add play without changing the product or controls.

    Intended audience effect (hypothesis): The expressive composition may make individuality and creativity more immediately visible while the shared image and message preserve the invitation to belong.

    Risk: The layering and contrast may feel too busy or less universally approachable, as the learner predicted.
